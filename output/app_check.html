Check 3Problem 9 usability feature: sticky "Add to cart" bar

PASS

What this proves: on an item page, a bar with the photo, price, stock, a size menu and Add to cart stays docked under the menu, so buying is always one click away. Nothing covers the product.

Also notedThe cheer banner stops when you hover over it

PASS

What this proves: the navy banner of Yale cheers ("Boola Boola", "Bulldogs, Bulldogs, Bow Wow Wow"…) scrolls on its own, and stops the moment the mouse is over it so a shopper can read it. The label switches from "Hover to pause" to "❚❚ Paused". It starts again when the mouse moves away (on phones, a tap pauses it). Small touches like this make the shop feel authentic and easy to use.
